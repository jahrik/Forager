### Item 2 (bubble re-anchors low after a rotation) - STOP at diagnosis; depends on MapLibre's projection

- **Governing rule:** Part 2 follow-ups F1 item 1 (`docs/audits/2026-09-29-part-2-followups-map-completion-report.md:165`): "open a photo/find bubble in portrait, rotate 0 to 90 and 90 to 0 with no pan; it sits on its glyph and clears the cluster".

- **What does not fit:** the photo bubble was a fan member; the re-anchor projects the photo's **true** position, which for a fanned copy differs from where it was tapped by a fan radius (about 135 px at 450 dpi), yet it moved 43 px. ## Decisions I made (Continuation)

- **Item 4:** I read "does not fit above its glyph" as "the card's unclamped top is above `minY`", so a card that overflows the top by a few px also flips (rather than only when the clamped card would cover the glyph). I flip only for the top edge, not the bottom, and only when the mirrored card fits entirely; both are mine.
- **Item 3:** the L's inset is **added to** the rail/cut-out padding on the same side, because the L sits inside `controlsPadding` (`MapIconCluster` applies it first); the ruling names the two values but not their sum. The clamp box includes the tail's margin, so the visible card is a further 24 px inside; conservative, not exact.
- **Item 1:** the ruling says the "i" moves clear of the L "on whichever side the L is snapped to". Moving it whenever the L is on the "i"'s end edge would have changed 270, where the run record found no fail and where an existing test (`T5 guard at ROTATION_270`) and an earlier planner ruling keep the "i" at the edge. So I move it only when the two rectangles intersect (by the assumed size, 24 dp, and MapLibre's defaults read at runtime). That is a design choice, made so as not to change a state with no reported fault; the alternative (always inboard of an L on that edge) is one line.
- **Item 8:**
  - The request also holds the bubble's **anchor and bearing**; the ruling names only the find id and the fan keys. A bubble cannot be placed without an anchor, and the map's camera-idle re-anchor replaces it with the glyph's own position once the map is idle.
  - "Back from that find" is `onCloseEntry`, the report's close. Back from the **edit form** is `onLeaveEditingIncidentally`, a different path, and I left it as it was (it returns to the Journal), consistent with "editing then saving or discarding forgets".
  - The origin is forgotten on **any** non-Journal `compactTab`, on edit start, on the day-entry route, and when a different find closes. A request whose find never opened (`onOpenEntry` finding nothing) stays until one of those; I chose not to forget on "another id seen while waiting" because a find kept open on the Journal is left first and would look like one.
  - The request is session memory (`remember`, not saveable), like the camera: an Activity recreation forgets it and Back does what it did before.
  - The fan reopens at the **first idle after the style has loaded**, not at style load, for the reason in "Fold triggers".
  - A delete from the page returns to the Maps tab whether or not the delete is later undone.
- **Test infrastructure edits (in scope of item 8):** `MapLayersTestScreen` gained three optional parameters; `FanOutTestScene` implements the new `MapProbe.markersOf`. No assertion in either was changed.
