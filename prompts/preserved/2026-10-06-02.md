## Formats (the planner's proposal; argue with reasons rather than silently differ)

- **Elevation:** whole feet, metres ÷ 0.3048 exactly, rounded the way the metre figure is (`roundToInt`). Written " ft" with a space, as " m" is. The rise keeps its sign: "+33 ft", "-12 ft".
- **Soil temperature:** one decimal, °F = °C × 9/5 + 32, written the way today's site writes °C ("52.3°F", no space).
- **The guidance range:** whole °F, from the same constant (`FruitingPatternAssumptions.TEMPERATE_FRUITING_SOIL_TEMPERATURE_C`, 10.0..20.0, so 50–68). Keep the space the °C range has (" °F").
- `Locale.US` for every decimal point, as `formatRainfall` does.
- Put the formatters beside `formatRainfall` in `domain/model/UnitSystem.kt` as pure functions, testable headless.
