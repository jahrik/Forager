## Why

The own-tiles survey's seventh decision, accepted by the owner (`RECORD.md` -466): before any online switch, a device check that a labelled vector map renders on MapLibre Android 13.5.0 without the old crash. The survey (`docs/plans/2026-10-03-own-tiles-survey.md`, section 2 and "Premises ... wrong") found that the crash's record points at a section that does not contain it; the recorded claim is a native crash while building an **offline** pack's resource list for styles with label (glyph) layers, at any region size (`MapLibreOfflineMapRepository.kt:60-63`), and both docs call it offline-only (`docs/plans/pmtiles-worker-android-wiring.md:177-179`), with no test of a labelled vector style online ever recorded. The owner chose "Proceed with 1 to 3", this check being 2. Its answer shapes the spec (stage 3 of `docs/plans/trail-navigation-and-own-tiles.md`).

## What to measure

On the S22, with MapLibre Android **13.5.0** exactly as Forager pins it, in a separate test app (own `applicationId`), loading `map-style/forager-light.json` from the scaffold, with its fonts and icons served from the laptop's `map-style/` server and reached from the phone over `adb reverse` (or another way that sends nothing beyond the laptop), and tiles from the old Worker's `/us.json` (not the Pi):
1. **Online:** the style loads and draws labels and icons at a town, a national forest and a coastline zoom, without a crash; logcat for native errors; screenshots.
2. **An offline pack:** download a small region (a few km², z10 to z15) with this labelled style through `OfflineManager`, as Forager does for its regions (`MapLibreOfflineMapRepository.kt`), and see whether the old crash happens: where, with what native trace. Then, in aeroplane mode, whether the pack draws with labels.
3. **If 2 crashes:** narrow it as far as cheaply possible (glyph ranges named by the style, the glyph URL's form, one symbol layer at a time), and say what that means for shipping labels offline. Do not fix Forager.
4. Anything about 13.5.0's rendering that differs visibly from the GL JS preview.
