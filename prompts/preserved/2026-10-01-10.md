1. **A tap on a fanned icon gives that marker's own outcome, as a tap on it would, and the fan folds, on that one tap.** For every kind a fan can hold.
2. **Every other tap with a fan open is unchanged:** another stack fans out and the old fan folds; a single marker gives its outcome and the fan folds; empty map folds the fan. Confirm these are so today and leave them.
3. **A bubble and a fan are then never up together.** So the order -255 item 7 set (a tap on empty map or a Back closes the bubble first, the next folds the fan) can no longer arise for a fan. Name every branch, flag and test that this leaves unreachable. Remove what is plainly dead, with its tests, and say what you removed; where you are not sure it is dead, leave it and list it.
4. **Back from a find's page reached from a fanned icon returns to the map with the same fan,** as the owner has it today. Since the fan is now gone by the time "Open in Journal" is pressed, the fan the find was picked from must still be what comes back: the same members, less any that no longer exist, by the rule `openFanFor` already has. A find reached from a bubble that did not come from a fan returns to the map with no fan, as today.
5. **The bubble points at the marker where it is.** Once the fan has folded, the tapped marker is back in its stack; a bubble left pointing at the place its fanned icon used to be is wrong. Say where the bubble is anchored today and where it ends up.
6. **Back with a fan open still folds it,** on screen or off; unchanged.
