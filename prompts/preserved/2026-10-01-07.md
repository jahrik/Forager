## The planner's diagnosis, to confirm or disprove
"Confirm this is the mechanism, or show where it is wrong."
- **A. The symbol fade.** MapLibre fades a newly placed symbol in over the style's transition duration, about 300 ms by default. Each per-frame `setGeoJson` on the icons source makes the copies new symbols, so their fade keeps restarting while the fan moves and only completes after it stops. This fits the fade-in after the fan is in place. It is inferred from how the library behaves, not read from its source here and not observed.
- **B. Sources out of step.** The four sources are re-tiled separately and off the main thread, so one frame can show the icons' new data with the circles' old or empty data, and the next the reverse. This would fit the alternation. Also inferred.
- **Not yet explained by either:** why one layer's glyphs stay solid in one clip and dim in the other.

Other candidates the planner considered and has **not** ruled out: the push rate (120 Hz on the S26, 60 on the S22) outrunning the re-tiling; `symbolSortKey` (-318) forcing a re-placement; `-299`'s per-feature offset and circle scale changing a property on every frame.

**Two failed hypotheses mean you instrument instead of guessing a third** (CLAUDE.md).

- **Fold it,** each way a fan can be folded: the markers return with no empty moment and no doubled icon.
- **Interrupt it:** press Back the instant a fan starts to open, and, if another stack is in view, tap it while a fan is open. No marker left missing or doubled.
- **The same on the S26.**
