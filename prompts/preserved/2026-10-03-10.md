## Why

-457 made a nudge under 48 dp (`NAVIGATION_NUDGE_THRESHOLD_DP`) leave tracking untouched through MapLibre's `trackingGesturesManagement`. As built, the map does not move at all for such a nudge. The owner, verbatim: "When nudging it add a slight elastic snap to show the user that the nudge was corrected." Told that the map must give a little first for a snap to be seen, the owner chose "Give, then spring back", to this step path (`RECORD.md` -463):

1. While navigating, the walker nudges the map: it moves with the finger but stiffly, about half as far, as if on a rubber band.
2. Lifted before the drag threshold: it springs back to following with a slight overshoot and settle, in about a quarter of a second.
3. Dragged past the threshold: it lets go, stops following, and "Return to Route" appears, as now.
4. A pinch still zooms and keeps following.
