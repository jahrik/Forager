## What to measure, for each engine

On the S22, offline (aeroplane mode for the routing step), with OpenStreetMap data for Oregon:
1. **Data on the phone:** the routing data's size for Oregon (and, from the files, what Oregon plus Washington would be).
2. **App size:** what the engine adds to an APK, per ABI.
3. **Route time and memory:** three foot or hiking routes of about 10 km along trails in a national forest in Oregon, each run five times: time to the first route, and peak memory.
4. **Starting off the trail:** a start 200 m from the nearest trail. What each engine returns: whether it snaps, how far, and whether it returns the connecting segment.
5. **Hiking awareness:** whether each route stays on trails a walker would use, and how `sac_scale` and informal trails affect it, read from the route against the map. Observations, not a pass condition.
6. **Integration notes:** what running each inside the app would take (BRouter's library in-process is undocumented; say whether it works), and its licence as shipped.

Pick routes that exist on both engines' data. Put the routes' endpoints in the evidence folder only, never in the repository: it is public and they are real places.
