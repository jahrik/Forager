## The behaviour, as the owner confirmed it (RECORD -515)

You start recording and walk out. The app keeps working out the **leave-by time**: sunset, minus the darkness margin (default one hour, `SundownPreferencesRepository`), minus the walk back along the track at the walker's moving pace (`returnWalkingTime`, `domain/ReturnWalkingTime.kt`, on GPS pace, `domain/MovingPace.kt`). It moves earlier the further out the walker goes.

1. **30 minutes before the leave-by time:** a heads-up alert.
2. **At the leave-by time:** a second alert.
3. **At sunset:** the sunset alert.

If the walker is already past the leave-by time when recording starts, or the estimate moves it into the past, the leave-by alert fires at once (and the heads-up is spent without firing, as `DecideSundownAlertUseCase` already does for a turnaround passed before sunset fires). Each alert fires once per recording. All three sound through a silenced phone (owner, 2026-09-11). Recording only.

**The alerts inform; they never order the walker back.** The owner, verbatim: "instead of 'turn around now' just tell them when sundown is. A lot of people do night forays for bioluminescence." Today's turnaround copy, "Time to head back" (`res/values/strings.xml:13`), goes. Provisional copy, for you to improve and the owner to approve in your report:

- Heads-up: **"Sunset at 7:12 PM"** / "The walk back is about 1 h 30. Leaving in 30 min gets you back before dark."
- Leave-by: **"Sunset at 7:12 PM"** / "The walk back is about 1 h 30. Leaving now gets you back before dark."
- Sunset: unchanged, unless it reads as an order.

When the estimate is "at least X" it says "at least 1 h 30". When walking time is withheld (`ReturnWalkingTime.Withheld`: no fix, no usable points), the leave-by time falls back to sunset minus the margin and the text says the walk back is unknown. **Never a confident figure the estimate does not have.** The 30 minutes is fixed, not a setting (owner, -515). "Before dark" means before sunset minus the margin; if that reading is wrong for the copy, say so.
