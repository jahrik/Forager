> "Open Tools, then press Back. Does the search dropdown pop open by itself? No"
> "Open the search dropdown, then press Back. Does it close and stay closed? No"

**So:** Back does not dismiss the Maps tab's search dropdown. Closing the Tools drawer does **not** reopen it.

**What -298 found in the Robolectric harness** (`fan-back-others`, `bafa0fde`; continuation -303 in `RECORD.md`), from a stack trace:
- after the dropdown closes, `AndroidComposeView.requestFocus` hands focus back to the search field;
- `onFieldFocused = { showSearchDropdown = true }` (`ui/availability/AvailabilityCompactScaffold.kt:838`) opens it again;
- a test with no fan (open the dropdown, press Back, assert it closed) fails there. `LeavingTheJournalFixesTest`'s F4 dropdown test closes it in its own harness, and nobody knows why the two differ.
- In the same harness the drawer case also reopened the dropdown, but on the device it does not. **So the harness and the device differ on the drawer, and the harness is not trusted blindly.**

**The rule.** Back with the search dropdown open closes it, and it stays closed until the user opens it again by touching the search field. Nothing else about the dropdown changes: what opens it, what it shows, and how picking a result or "Use current location" closes it.
