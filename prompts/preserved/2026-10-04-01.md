## Amendment 1 (2026-10-04, at step 9): how the Worker will reach the Pi

**Found by the Pi's session before step 10:** Cloudflare's Workers VPC is in beta and recommends a dashboard-managed tunnel; a public-hostname tunnel and a VPC service are different ingress models.

**The owner chose,** verbatim, "Pi hidden behind the Worker" (`RECORD.md` -479):
- The public name `tiles.zynergy-labs.com` will belong to the **Worker** (next part): it caches, limits abuse and falls back to R2 when the Pi is down. Keep `--public-url https://tiles.zynergy-labs.com` on the tile server; the tiles' URLs in its TileJSON are the public ones.
- The **tunnel's hostname is a separate origin name,** `origin.zynergy-labs.com` unless the owner names another in your window. Use it in steps 10 to 12 instead of `tiles.zynergy-labs.com`.
- **Only the Worker may use the origin name:** a Cloudflare Access application on the origin hostname with a service-token policy (the Worker will send the token's two headers in the next part). Creating the Access application and the service token is a change on the owner's account: ask in your window, and hand the owner any dashboard step. The token's secret never enters the repository or a report.
- **Locally-managed tunnel, as ruled.** No Workers VPC.
- **Step 12's check** then becomes: through the origin hostname, a request without the token is refused by Access, and a request with it (from the Pi, the token read from a root-only file) returns `/us.json` and a z5 tile. Report both.
