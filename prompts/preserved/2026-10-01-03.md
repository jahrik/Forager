# Dispatch 2026-09-28-358: trip-flag-size, the planned-trip flag drawn 23 dp tall

**The owner, verbatim,** after looking at an open fan on their S26 and at the 36 dp circles:
> "The circle size was perfect at 36dp since the trip icon fit neatly in it"

> "Okay never mind, it's fine as is. But one quick thing, the trip icon is a bit large still. Scale it down very slightly so that it fits more comfortably inside the circle."

**The number is the planner's, not the owner's.** The owner said "very slightly" and gave no size. The planner chose **23 dp tall** and told the owner so. If the owner changes it before you build, the planner will message you; do not pick another number yourself.

**Why 23, the planner's arithmetic, to verify:** the circle's radius is 18 dp. A fan copy is centred on its fill extent (fan-centring, -275). With the 1.5 dp casing, the flag's farthest corner from the circle's centre is `sqrt((10s)^2 + (14s)^2) + 1.5` dp, where `s` is the flag's scale.
- Today, `s = 25/28`: about 16.9 dp, so about 1.1 dp of circle is left at the corners.
- At `s = 23/28`: about 15.6 dp, so about 2.4 dp is left. That is close to what the pin has at its top and its tip (2.5 dp).

**The rule.** Scale the PLANNED_TRIP shape **evenly**, with its proportions kept, to **23 dp tall**.
- 20 × 28 becomes about 16.43 × 23, a scale of 23/28.
- The anchor scales with the shape, so it stays on the pole foot: (1.5, 28) becomes about (1.23, 23).
- **Scope: everywhere,** as the owner ruled for -286 ("Everywhere"): one size for the flag on the map, in the fan, and anywhere else the glyph is drawn.

**Unchanged:** FIND (23.08 × 25, still 25 dp tall), WAYPOINT (22 × 28), PHOTO (22 × 22) and SEARCH_CENTRE; the casing width and every detail stroke, which do not scale; the colours; the circle (36 dp); the touch area and the fan's spacing (48 dp); the stack distance (26 dp).
