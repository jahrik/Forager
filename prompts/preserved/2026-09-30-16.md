The bitmap is padded so that its centre is the glyph's anchor (`MarkerGlyphs.kt`, `drawGlyph`), so **the anchor sits on the coordinate**, for example the pin's tip or the find's stem foot.
- **The fan copy:** `addFanOutLayers`'s symbol layer (`FanOutLayers.kt:85-93`) is also `CENTER`, plus `iconOffset(get(ICON_OFFSET_PROPERTY))`. `fanFrameCollections` (`FanOutLayers.kt:153-196`) writes that property as `glyph.fanCentringOffsetDp()` (`:176`), the same at **every** progress. `fanCentringOffsetDp()` (`MarkerGlyphs.kt:87`) is `(anchorX − width/2, anchorY − height/2)`, which puts the **body's centre** on the point.
- **So at progress 0 the copy sits off from its original by exactly `fanCentringOffsetDp()`,** and snaps by that amount when `release()` clears the copies (`MarkerFanOutState.kt:59-63`, driven by `MarkerFanOutHost`, `:73-86`).
