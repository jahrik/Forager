## Why

Indoors on the S22, both main's build and -502's showed "Location services unavailable" for two minutes while the phone had a network location good to about 100 m, which the app's existing 50 m gate refuses (`RECORD.md` -508). The owner, verbatim: "We should have a way to grab location just to establish a position, so users aren't stuck waiting for something." The owner chose, each the planner's recommendation (-508):

1. **No precise fix yet:** the map shows an approximate position (a soft dot in a circle sized to its reported accuracy, labelled "Approximate location") and centres there.
2. **The HUD while navigating:** a far target's distance shows as "≈ …" with the needle; when the target is inside the circle of uncertainty, the needle is hidden and the HUD reads "Approximate, finding GPS…".
3. **Never decides anything:** an approximate reading never enters a recorded track, the off-track alert, "Arrived", or anything else that acts on position.
4. **GPS takes over** the moment a precise fix arrives: the circle shrinks to the normal dot and the label goes, with nothing for the walker to do.
5. **No location at all:** the last known position shows, greyed, with its age ("Last seen 2 h ago"), until anything newer arrives.
